# LCOfficeV81UnofficialCompatibilityFixCleanupFix

A cleanup fix for **LC_Office** when using **LC Office v81 Unofficial Compatibility Fix** on Lethal Company v81.
The compatibility patch replaces LC Office's original initialization without restoring its ship-leaving cleanup listener. Elevator and storage objects can then survive an interior change. This can leave behind collision, player physics regions and pathfinding links, while `ElevatorStorage.LateUpdate` repeatedly accesses a destroyed transform.
LC Office Cleanup restores cleanup at ship departure and adds fallback cleanup before the next level loads.


## Problem

When using LC_Office and LC Office v81 Unofficial Compatibility Fix after you leave LC-Office once you will encounter weird elevator behavior on all subsequent moons.
Weird behavior means:
- Invisible Walls/Weird Hitboxes
- Glitching Elevators
- Falling through elevator/map


## Features

- Removes leftover LC Office elevator and storage objects.
- Disables their collision, player physics regions and navigation obstacles before disposal.
- Unregisters the old elevator's PathfindingLib floor links.
- Detaches players from retired elevator physics transforms.
- Stops storage updates from repeatedly accessing a destroyed target.
- Uses server-authoritative network despawning with local cleanup on clients.


## Technical Details

- Developed for Lethal Company v81, LC_Office 2.3.4 and LC Office v81 Unofficial Compatibility Fix 2.0.0
- **Install on the host and all clients.** Keep LC Office and its v81 compatibility patch enabled.**
- This add-on targets the cleanup behavior of the versions listed above. It does not replace either required mod or provide a general fix for every elevator, interior collision or enemy AI issue. Multiplayer behavior with other mod combinations may vary.


## Installation

- Install through a Thunderstore-compatible mod manager, or copy `plugins/LCOfficeCleanup.dll` from the release ZIP into your profile's `BepInEx/plugins` directory.
- Restart the game after installation. There are no configuration options. The startup log reports `LC Office Cleanup 0.1.0 active` when the fix is loaded.
- To uninstall a manual installation, remove `LCOfficeCleanup.dll` and restart the game.
